# Product

## Register

product

## Users

This is a personal utility for quick QR generation during everyday development and sharing work. The user arrives with a text value, URL, or identifier already in mind and wants a reliable QR image with minimal ceremony.

## Product Purpose

The product turns arbitrary text into downloadable PNG and SVG QR codes. Success means the encoded content is visible, export actions are obvious when ready, and the interface stays fast, predictable, and readable.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, accessible. The interface should feel like a dependable tool: restrained, direct, and clear under repeated use.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page theatrics, decorative SaaS gradients, overbuilt onboarding, playful QR gimmicks, and UI patterns that slow down generation or export. Do not make the tool feel like a campaign page.

## Design Principles

- Keep generation first: input, preview, and export should remain the dominant workflow.
- Make state explicit: empty, generating, ready, and failed states should be visually and semantically distinct.
- Prefer earned familiarity: standard form controls, clear buttons, and predictable layout beat novelty.
- Preserve readability: meet WCAG contrast targets and keep text legible on small screens.
- Use restraint deliberately: color and motion should clarify state or action, not decorate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for text contrast, focus visibility, touch targets, and keyboard access. Support reduced motion whenever motion is introduced. Do not rely on color alone for ready, disabled, or error states.
